~/about

Engineer at the intersection of research and production.

I'm a Machine Learning Engineer working across the full model lifecycle — data exploration and feature engineering, training and fine-tuning, evaluation, deployment, and the drift monitoring that happens long after launch day. Most of my work lives in generative AI and agentic systems: orchestrating LLM agents that handle real request volume, building retrieval pipelines that hold up outside a notebook, and putting guardrails around models that operate with less supervision than anyone's fully comfortable with.

Currently I'm at Pioneerstek, architecting multi-agent orchestration systems and fine-tuning domain models with LoRA/PEFT. Before that, I built routing and RAG infrastructure at Sirch, and before that, real-time order-routing models for institutional trading at Blockhouse — a route into ML that started, unusually, in mechanical engineering.

The unglamorous half of the job — eval harnesses, drift monitors, rollback systems — is the half I care about most. It's the difference between a demo and something you can leave running.
